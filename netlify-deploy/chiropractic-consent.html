Consent to Treatment

Posture Clinic — Chiropractic care and acupuncture informed consent

Clinic and purpose

This informed consent is provided by Posture Clinic (the “Clinic”). It explains chiropractic assessment and treatment so you can decide whether to proceed. It does not replace discussion with your chiropractor about your specific condition and plan of care.

Nature of care

Chiropractic care at Posture Clinic may include history-taking, examination, orthopedic and neurological testing, imaging referral when appropriate, spinal and extremity joint adjustment/manipulation and mobilization, soft-tissue techniques, therapeutic modalities, exercise and rehabilitation advice, lifestyle advice, and related clinical services within the chiropractor’s scope of practice.

Expected benefits

Potential benefits may include reduced pain, improved mobility/function, and support for recovery from neuromusculoskeletal conditions. Results vary. No cure or specific outcome is guaranteed.

Material risks and side effects

As with any health care, chiropractic treatment involves risk. Common temporary effects can include soreness, stiffness, fatigue, headache, or short-term aggravation of symptoms. Less common effects may include dizziness or temporary numbness/tingling. Rare but serious complications associated with certain procedures (including some neck treatments) have been reported in the scientific literature, including stroke-related events, disc injury, fracture, or nerve injury in susceptible patients. Your chiropractor will discuss risks that apply to you.

Alternatives

Alternatives may include no treatment, rest, self-care, exercise, medication prescribed by a physician, physiotherapy, massage therapy, osteopathy, acupuncture, injection therapies, surgery, or referral to another provider. You may seek a second opinion.

Consequences of declining care

If you decline recommended assessment or treatment, your condition may stay the same, worsen, or improve on its own. Delaying care may prolong symptoms or limit options in some cases.

Your responsibilities

You agree to provide complete and accurate health information (including medications, prior injuries, surgeries, pregnancy, blood clotting disorders, bone density issues, and other relevant history) and to update the Clinic if your health changes. Incomplete information may affect safety and outcomes.

Privacy and records

Your personal health information will be collected, used, and disclosed as needed for your care, clinic operations, billing/insurance where authorized, and as required or permitted by law. Records are kept confidential subject to applicable privacy legislation.

Consent, questions, and withdrawal

Consent must be informed. You may ask questions at any time. You may refuse any procedure or withdraw consent at any time without affecting your right to future care (subject to clinical and legal limits). This online acknowledgment is evidence of informed consent for intake purposes; your chiropractor will review care with you in person as appropriate, and additional consent may be required for specific procedures.

Important: This form does not create a guarantee of results, does not waive the Clinic’s professional obligations, and does not replace emergency medical care. If you have sudden severe symptoms (for example chest pain, sudden neurological change, or trauma), seek emergency medical attention.

Acupuncture and related procedures

If acupuncture is recommended as part of your care at Posture Clinic, treatment may include sterile disposable needling, electroacupuncture, moxibustion, cupping, heat lamp, and related adjunctive procedures as clinically indicated and permitted.

Material risks of acupuncture include bruising, bleeding, local pain/numbness/tingling, dizziness or fainting, temporary symptom aggravation, burns/scarring from heat or cupping, infection, nerve injury, and rare organ puncture including lung puncture (pneumothorax). Results are not guaranteed. Acupuncture is not a substitute for medical care when medically indicated. Tell staff if you are pregnant, nursing, take anticoagulants, have a bleeding disorder, pacemaker/implanted device, or other relevant conditions.

Alternatives include no acupuncture, self-care, medication, physiotherapy, chiropractic/osteopathic care, injections, surgery, or other options. You may refuse acupuncture or withdraw consent at any time.

Patient acknowledgment

By accepting below and checking the consent box on the intake form, I confirm that I have read (or had read to me) this Consent to Treatment for Posture Clinic, including chiropractic care and acupuncture if recommended, understand the nature of care, material risks, alternatives, and my right to ask questions and withdraw consent, and I consent to evaluation and treatment by Posture Clinic, subject to further discussion with my practitioner. I confirm the information I provide on the intake form is accurate to the best of my knowledge.

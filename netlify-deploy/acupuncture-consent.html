Consent to Acupuncture Treatment

Posture Clinic — Informed consent for acupuncture and related procedures

Clinic and purpose

This informed consent is provided by Posture Clinic (the “Clinic”). Informed consent means you understand the recommended care, expected benefits, material risks and side effects, alternatives, and likely consequences of declining care, and that you agree voluntarily. Acupuncture is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment by a medical doctor when medical care is needed.

Nature of care

I request and consent to acupuncture treatments and related procedures within the applicable scope of practice performed at Posture Clinic by the treating practitioner(s) and authorized clinical staff. Methods may include, but are not limited to: acupuncture needling with sterile disposable needles; electroacupuncture; moxibustion; cupping; heat lamp; Tui-Na / soft-tissue techniques; and related adjunctive procedures as clinically indicated and permitted.

Expected benefits

Potential benefits may include reduced pain, improved function, and support for recovery. Results vary. No cure or specific outcome is guaranteed.

Material risks and side effects

Acupuncture is generally considered safe when performed with sterile technique, but risks include: bruising; bleeding; local pain, numbness, or tingling near needling sites (which may last a few days); dizziness or fainting; and temporary aggravation of symptoms. Burns and/or scarring are potential risks of moxibustion, cupping, or heat lamps. Bruising is a common side effect of cupping. Unusual risks include infection, nerve injury, and organ puncture, including lung puncture (pneumothorax). The Clinic uses sterile disposable needles and maintains infection-control practices; residual risk cannot be eliminated entirely.

Pregnancy, medical conditions, and disclosure

I will notify clinical staff if I am or become pregnant, am nursing, have a bleeding disorder, take anticoagulant medication, have a pacemaker or implanted device, have a metal allergy, have an infection, or have any other condition that may affect treatment safety. I will continue to fully inform the Clinic of my medical history, medications, and supplements.

Alternatives

Alternatives may include no treatment, self-care, over-the-counter pain relief, rest, physiotherapy, chiropractic or osteopathic care, medical care with prescription drugs, injections, surgery, or other options. I understand I may seek a second opinion.

Consequences of declining care

If I decline acupuncture, my condition may stay the same, worsen, or improve without this care.

Privacy and records

Clinical and administrative staff may review my records as needed for care. Records are kept confidential and are not released without my authorization except as required or permitted by law.

Consent, questions, and withdrawal

I understand results are not guaranteed. I may ask questions and may refuse or withdraw consent at any time. Sensitive-area treatment, if ever proposed, requires separate discussion and consent as required by applicable standards. This online acknowledgment is evidence of informed consent for intake purposes and is intended to cover the course of acupuncture care for my present and future conditions for which I seek treatment at Posture Clinic, unless I withdraw consent.

Important: This consent is for care provided by Posture Clinic. It does not waive professional standards of care. Seek emergency medical attention for sudden severe symptoms.

Patient acknowledgment

By accepting below and checking the acupuncture consent box on the intake form, I confirm that I have read (or had read to me) this Consent to Acupuncture Treatment for Posture Clinic, have been informed of benefits, material risks, and alternatives, have had an opportunity to ask questions, and I consent to acupuncture and related procedures as described above.

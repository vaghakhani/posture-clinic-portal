Consent to Treatment

Posture Clinic — Chiropractic, Osteopathy, and Acupuncture informed consent

Clinic and purpose

This informed consent is provided by Posture Clinic (the “Clinic”), located at 712 Davis Dr, Newmarket, ON L3Y 8C3. It explains chiropractic care, osteopathic care, and acupuncture so you can decide whether to proceed. It does not replace discussion with your treating practitioner about your specific condition and plan of care.

A. Chiropractic care

Chiropractic care may include history-taking, examination, orthopedic and neurological testing, imaging referral when appropriate, spinal and extremity joint adjustment/manipulation and mobilization, soft-tissue techniques, therapeutic modalities, exercise and rehabilitation advice, and related clinical services within the chiropractor’s scope of practice.

Potential benefits may include reduced pain, improved mobility/function, and support for recovery from neuromusculoskeletal conditions. Results vary. No cure or specific outcome is guaranteed.

Material risks may include temporary soreness, stiffness, fatigue, headache, or short-term aggravation of symptoms. Less common effects may include dizziness or temporary numbness/tingling. Rare but serious complications associated with certain procedures (including some neck treatments) have been reported, including stroke-related events, disc injury, fracture, or nerve injury in susceptible patients.

B. Osteopathic care

Osteopathic care may include history-taking, physical examination, postural and functional assessment, soft-tissue techniques, joint mobilization, muscle energy and related manual techniques, stretching, exercise advice, and other conservative musculoskeletal approaches within the practitioner’s training and applicable law. Techniques involving the head, neck, spine, pelvis, and extremities may be used when clinically appropriate.

Potential benefits may include reduced pain or tension, improved mobility/function, and support for recovery from musculoskeletal complaints. Results vary. No cure or specific outcome is guaranteed.

Material risks may include soreness, stiffness, fatigue, mild headache, or short-term aggravation of symptoms. Less common effects may include dizziness, bruising, or temporary numbness/tingling. Rare complications of manual therapy can include injury to soft tissue, joints, discs, nerves, or vessels, or fracture in susceptible patients. Tell your practitioner about osteoporosis, anticoagulant use, recent surgery, pregnancy, cancer history, infection, unexplained weight loss, night pain, trauma, or neurological symptoms.

C. Acupuncture and related procedures

If acupuncture is recommended as part of your care at Posture Clinic, treatment may include sterile disposable needling, electroacupuncture, moxibustion, cupping, heat lamp, Tui-Na / soft-tissue techniques, and related adjunctive procedures as clinically indicated and permitted.

Material risks include bruising, bleeding, local pain/numbness/tingling, dizziness or fainting, temporary symptom aggravation, burns/scarring from heat or cupping, infection, nerve injury, and rare organ puncture including lung puncture (pneumothorax). Results are not guaranteed. Acupuncture is not a substitute for medical care when medically indicated. Tell staff if you are pregnant, nursing, take anticoagulants, have a bleeding disorder, pacemaker/implanted device, metal allergy, or other relevant conditions.

Alternatives and declining care

Alternatives may include no treatment, rest, self-care, exercise, medication prescribed by a physician, physiotherapy, massage therapy, injection therapies, surgery, or referral to another provider. You may seek a second opinion. If you decline recommended assessment or treatment, your condition may stay the same, worsen, or improve on its own.

Your responsibilities, privacy, and withdrawal

You agree to provide complete and accurate health information and to update the Clinic if your health changes. Your personal health information will be collected, used, and disclosed as needed for your care, clinic operations, billing/insurance where authorized, and as required or permitted by law. You may ask questions at any time. You may refuse any procedure or withdraw consent at any time. Additional consent may be required for specific procedures, including sensitive-area treatment where applicable.

Important: This form does not create a guarantee of results, does not waive the Clinic’s professional obligations, and does not replace emergency medical care. If you have sudden severe symptoms (for example chest pain, sudden neurological change, or trauma), seek emergency medical attention. Contact: 647-835-8118.

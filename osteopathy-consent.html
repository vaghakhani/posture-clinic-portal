Consent to Treatment

Posture Clinic — Osteopathic care and acupuncture informed consent

Clinic and purpose

This informed consent is provided by Posture Clinic (the “Clinic”). It explains osteopathic assessment and manual treatment so you can decide whether to proceed. It does not replace discussion with your osteopathic practitioner about your specific condition and plan of care.

Nature of care

Osteopathic care at Posture Clinic may include history-taking, physical examination, postural and functional assessment, soft-tissue techniques, joint mobilization, muscle energy and related manual techniques, stretching, exercise advice, and other conservative musculoskeletal approaches within the practitioner’s training and applicable law. Techniques involving the head, neck, spine, pelvis, and extremities may be used when clinically appropriate.

Expected benefits

Potential benefits may include reduced pain or tension, improved mobility/function, and support for recovery from musculoskeletal complaints. Results vary. No cure or specific outcome is guaranteed.

Material risks and side effects

Manual osteopathic treatment involves risk. Common temporary effects can include soreness, stiffness, fatigue, mild headache, or short-term aggravation of symptoms. Less common effects may include dizziness, bruising, or temporary numbness/tingling. Rare complications of manual therapy can include injury to soft tissue, joints, discs, nerves, or vessels, or fracture in susceptible patients. Please tell your practitioner about osteoporosis, anticoagulant use, recent surgery, pregnancy, cancer history, infection, unexplained weight loss, night pain, trauma, or neurological symptoms.

Alternatives

Alternatives may include no treatment, rest, self-care, exercise, medication prescribed by a physician, physiotherapy, chiropractic care, massage therapy, acupuncture, injection therapies, surgery, or referral to another provider. You may seek a second opinion.

Consequences of declining care

If you decline recommended assessment or treatment, your condition may stay the same, worsen, or improve on its own. Delaying care may prolong symptoms in some cases.

Your responsibilities

You agree to provide complete and accurate health information and to update the Clinic if your health changes. Incomplete information may affect safety and outcomes.

Privacy and records

Your personal health information will be collected, used, and disclosed as needed for your care, clinic operations, billing/insurance where authorized, and as required or permitted by law.

Consent, questions, and withdrawal

You may ask questions at any time. You may refuse any technique or withdraw consent at any time. This online acknowledgment is evidence of informed consent for intake purposes; your practitioner will review care with you in person as appropriate, and additional consent may be required for specific procedures.

Important: Osteopathic care at Posture Clinic is not a substitute for emergency medical care or physician diagnosis when medically indicated. If you have sudden severe symptoms, seek emergency medical attention.

Acupuncture and related procedures

If acupuncture is recommended as part of your care at Posture Clinic, treatment may include sterile disposable needling, electroacupuncture, moxibustion, cupping, heat lamp, and related adjunctive procedures as clinically indicated and permitted.

Material risks of acupuncture include bruising, bleeding, local pain/numbness/tingling, dizziness or fainting, temporary symptom aggravation, burns/scarring from heat or cupping, infection, nerve injury, and rare organ puncture including lung puncture (pneumothorax). Results are not guaranteed. Acupuncture is not a substitute for medical care when medically indicated. Tell staff if you are pregnant, nursing, take anticoagulants, have a bleeding disorder, pacemaker/implanted device, or other relevant conditions.

Alternatives include no acupuncture, self-care, medication, physiotherapy, chiropractic/osteopathic care, injections, surgery, or other options. You may refuse acupuncture or withdraw consent at any time.
